25% Uttarakhand Domicile

Uttarakhand State Domicile Scholarship

The Uttaranchal University awards 25% scholarship on Tuition Fee to Uttarakhand Domicile Students. 25% of seats for these students are reserved for the entire programmes of the University.

To claim this scholarship, the student must carry a valid Domicile Certificate (Original) of Uttarakhand State at the time of admission/counselling.

5% Scholarship for Girls

Sushila Devi Scholarship for Girls

The Uttaranchal University awards 5% Sushila Devi Scholarship for Girl students on net Tuition Fee. This scholarship is applicable for the entire duration of programme. The Scholarship percentage will remain same for "Uttarakhand"/"All India" category.

Note: No Scholarship is available for MBA Executive, B.Sc. Nursing, Diploma in Hotel Management & Certificate in Hotel Management.

25% Hill States Domicile

Hills States Domicile Scholarship

The Uttaranchal University awards 25% scholarship on Tuition Fee to the Domiciled students of Hill States of India.

Eligible States & Regions: Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Nagaland, Assam, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Himachal Pradesh and District Darjeeling, Kalimpong (West Bengal).
